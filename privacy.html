Floof-Mon Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-23

Floof-Mon is a browser game. This policy explains what it stores and why.

What we store on our server

  • A game account ID. Created automatically when you install, so your progress can be saved. You can optionally link a Google account; we then also store the email address Google provides, used only to sign you in on other browsers.
  • Your display name (chosen by you, shown on leaderboards).
  • Game progress: the cats you caught, their levels, your items, your battle results, and your spawning on/off setting.

What we never collect

  • The pages you visit. Floof-Mon runs on web pages only to show cats. It never sends page addresses, page content, or browsing history anywhere.
  • Your "No-Floof zones" list, sound and theme settings stay in your browser and are never uploaded.

Who can see your data

Other players can see only your display name, your companion cat and your leaderboard score. We don't sell or share data with anyone. Data is hosted with Supabase.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling removes all local data. To delete your server account and progress, contact the developer at the email address listed on the Chrome Web Store page.
