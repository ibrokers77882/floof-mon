Wild cats are hiding on the internet.

Floof-Mon is a free browser extension. Cute hand-drawn cats sneak onto the pages you read. Battle them, catch them and collect them all.

How it works

🌐

Browse like normal

Every 10–15 minutes a wild Floof sneaks onto the page you're on.

🐾

Battle it

Pounce to lower its HP while it swipes back. Win and it's yours, every time.

✨

Grab items

Items sparkle on pages every few minutes: Yarn Balls, Catnip Potions, Fluff Balls and more.

📖

Collect them all

17 hand-drawn cats across 6 rarities, each with its own entrance animation.

⭐

Level up

Your companion levels up with every battle and gets better item drops.

🔒

Private by design

Floof-Mon never reads or sends the pages you visit. Pause it any time with one switch.
